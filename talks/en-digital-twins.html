Digital Twins

Credible virtual models across domains

Dr. Fabian Franzelin · 2026-09-24

Talk · W2-Professorship Computer Science · Reutlingen University

Introduction

Reliable testing of the application code of a release candidate of a robotaxi platform at high scale, with 13,500 h of recomputed data within 7 days.​

  1. Applications are exposed to uncontrollable uncertainties
  2. The digital twin is a reliable, virtual model of the vehicle
  3. Use Uncertainty Quantification (UQ) to close the reality gap
  4. Run representative test scenarios on the digital twin at scale in the cloud
