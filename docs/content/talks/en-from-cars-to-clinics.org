* Research profile at Reutlingen University

Starting from my established work on safety-critical distributed systems, I see
four accessible directions for applied research, student projects, and industry
collaboration across the university.

** Digital Twins

- *Original challenge:* How can we validate fleet software across thousands of
  changing vehicle configurations before deployment on the road?
- *My experience:* Led a Bosch/ETH digital-twin platform from research funding
  to series-ready development; two patents on distributed test infrastructure.
- *Clinical direction:* Apply virtual integration and reproducible regression
  tests to networked medical-device configurations before patient use.

** Trustworthy data

- *Original challenge:* How can uncertainty from heterogeneous data sources be
  quantified and propagated through large-scale simulations?
- *My experience:* PhD research in data-driven uncertainty quantification, plus
  clinical gait-sensor studies with multiple-sclerosis patients.
- *Clinical direction:* Make uncertainty visible in sensor-fusion pipelines for
  prevention, rehabilitation, and personalised care.

** Safe Engineering

- *Original challenge:* How can a safety case remain traceable while five agile
  teams continuously evolve distributed automotive software?
- *My experience:* Series responsibility under ISO 26262 and ASPICE, coordinating
  five agile teams developing safety-critical distributed software.
- *Clinical direction:* Adapt risk-based development and automated V&V to IEC
  62304 and ISO 14971 --- from research prototype to transferable product.
