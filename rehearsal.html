Demo failure mitigation

If localhost:8000 is not reachable:

  • The dashboard iframe silently falls back to slides/assets/demo-fallback.webm.
  • Presenter action: none. Do not draw attention.

If the whole demo pipeline fails:

  • Speaker note: "The demo is offline this morning — the sequence diagram behind me is what it would have shown." Move on.
